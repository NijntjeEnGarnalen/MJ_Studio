Mellow
AI Brand Identity, 2026
Special Thanks to Gerardo Herrera
Mellow Research Deck →

This project explores the design of calm, comfort-focused pet products for modern urban living, developed through an AI-driven workflow that integrates tools such as Flora, MidJourney, and Claude Code. From early-stage research synthesis to visual development and prototyping, AI was used not just for generation, but as a thinking partner, accelerating iteration, enabling rapid persona building, behavior mapping, and scenario visualization. Alongside this, we also built an AI brand agent to generate consistent voice and extend the brand across touchpoints.

Mabel Chen © 2026
