Miffy Trap
Font Design, 2026
Special Thanks to Hampton Dunlap

Miffy Trap is a contemporary sans serif defined by consistent strokes, open forms, and distinctive ink traps. Its clean structure is interrupted by sharp cuts, creating a balance between clarity and character. Designed to feel functional yet slightly unconventional, the typeface reveals more of its personality at larger scales.

Mabel Chen © 2026
